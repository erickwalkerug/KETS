KETS AUTOTRADER ENTRY UPDATE

Changed:
- AutoTrader entry direction still requires the existing rolling 100-record major-trend and configured pressure-ratio confirmation.
- New entries additionally require a swing-anchored Fixed Range Volume Profile POC retest and a confirmed post-retest ZigZag in the trend direction.
- Bullish: confirmed swing low -> swing high; downward approach and POC test; bullish higher-low/higher-high ZigZag; BUY.
- Bearish: confirmed swing high -> swing low; upward approach and POC test; bearish lower-high/lower-low ZigZag; SELL.
- Tick volume is distributed across 48 price bins as an approximation of volume-at-price. This is not exchange-traded real volume.
- Entries are blocked if sufficient OHLC and tick-volume candle data is not present in the order-flow records.

Unchanged:
- Existing AutoTrader exit logic, including the 0.05 drop from the peak pressure ratio.
- Existing major-trend 100-record calculation, pressure-ratio threshold, trade sizing, account handling, and other settings.

Deployment:
- This source-only ZIP excludes kets-android.apk and Python bytecode to keep the archive small.
- The incoming /api/order-flow records must include either a `candles` array of OHLC + tick_volume/volume candles, or one OHLC + tick_volume/volume candle per record. If the current external scanner sends only bullish_pressure/bearish_pressure and market_price, the new gate will safely wait and will not open trades until candle data is supplied.
