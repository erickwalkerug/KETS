SIMPLE AUTO-TRADE CONNECTION

The trading bot is the signal producer. It sends READY BUY/SELL signals directly
to this KETS website at /api/signals.

KETS then stores the signal in Signal History. The server-side Auto-Trade worker
reads that same history and sends orders to the connected cTrader account.

No separate signal server or KETS_SIGNAL_SOURCE_URL is required.

Expected flow:
Trading bot -> KETS website -> Signal History -> Auto-Trade -> cTrader

Manual trading remains separate and continues to use the website's manual-order
endpoint.

The auto worker is enabled by default in Render and the legacy source bridge is
disabled by default.
