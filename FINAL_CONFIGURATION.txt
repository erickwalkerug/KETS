- Signal History and 2-day retention
- Strong Reversal display/data
- SMC and additional strategy controls
- Auto-Trader controls and Signal History execution flow
- cTrader account selection, balance/profit and execution flow

- Temporary strategy-scan snapshots: 10-minute retention
- In-memory signal cache is bounded; persisted history remains 2 days
- cTrader account snapshots remain short-lived

The approved schedule is persisted in KETS app metadata so it survives normal restarts/redeploys when the configured persistent database storage is available.

No existing strategy thresholds or signal-generation rules were intentionally changed by this fix.
