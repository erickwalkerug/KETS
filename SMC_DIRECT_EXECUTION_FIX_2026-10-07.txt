KETS SMC DIRECT EXECUTION FIX — 2026-10-07

Purpose
- Make confirmed SMC signals from the trading bot eligible for direct cTrader execution.
- Do NOT recalculate SMC structure in KETS.
- Do NOT add a second candle/structure execution gate.
- Keep cTrader as the authority for the actual broker-side Stop Loss and Take Profit.

Execution flow
Trading Bot SMC evaluation
 -> KETS SMC ingress
 -> Auto-Trader existing controls
 -> cTrader market order
 -> cTrader receives the supplied SL + TP

Changes
1. SMC live signal lifetime increased from 180 seconds to 300 seconds so a normal polling/network delay does not unnecessarily expire a fresh signal.
2. KETS now recognizes explicit SMC source markers plus compatible SMC payload/source fields and SMC structural fields.
3. Added POST /api/smc-signals as a dedicated authenticated SMC ingress for bots that cannot add generic /api/signals source markers.
4. The dedicated SMC ingress normalizes the signal into the same canonical SMC identity used by Auto-Trader.
5. Existing Auto-Trader direction/reversal rules remain unchanged.
6. Existing SMC TP/SL broker-side enforcement remains unchanged. Automatic SMC orders still require valid TP and SL and cTrader confirmation.
7. No SMC strategy-generation logic was changed.

Important
- KETS does not decide whether liquidity sweep, displacement, BOS/CHOCH or order block is valid. Those SMC conditions remain the responsibility of the trading bot.
- The SMC Strategy switch must remain ON for SMC Auto-Trader entries.
- Auto-Trader must be ON and the cTrader account must be CONNECTED.
