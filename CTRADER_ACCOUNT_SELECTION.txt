KETS cTrader account selection

- KETS discovers all cTrader accounts authorized by the user.
- The user must explicitly choose an account and press "Use selected account for trading".
- KETS never silently selects the first account.
- Automatic and manual trading use only the selected account.
- Balance/equity/P&L dashboard data also uses the selected account.
- To add a newly created cTrader account, authorize KETS again through cTrader so the new account is included.
