GMMA Auto-Trader rules

  • BUY entry: the short-group average crosses above the long-group average on a completed candle.
  • SELL entry: the short-group average crosses below the long-group average on a completed candle.
  • Exit: close the existing KETS automatic position when a confirmed opposite GMMA group crossover occurs.
  • Safety: only completed candles are used; 61 candles are required to calculate the 60-period EMA and the feed must be fresh (maximum age 4 minutes). A confirmed BUY/SELL trend can trigger an entry without waiting for another crossover. On an opposite confirmed trend, the existing KETS automatic position is closed and the new direction is entered.

GMMA is a trend-following indicator and can lag or whipsaw in sideways markets. Test on demo before enabling live Auto-Trader.
