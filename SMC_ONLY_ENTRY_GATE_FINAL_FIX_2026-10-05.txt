KETS SMC-ONLY AUTO-TRADE — FINAL ENTRY GATE FIX — 2026-10-05

Root cause found:
- The previous KETS /api/signals POST handler unconditionally stamped every
  incoming BUY/SELL as:
    is_smc_signal=true
    signal_source=smc
    source_type=independent_smc
    strategy=smc
    strategy_name=smc
- That made unrelated signals appear to be valid SMC history entries.

Fixed:
1. /api/signals now preserves SMC identity only when the incoming payload
   explicitly identifies itself as SMC (or sends X-KETS-SIGNAL-SOURCE: smc).
2. Non-SMC signals are explicitly marked non-SMC and cannot satisfy the SMC
   history gate.
3. Auto-Trade is hard-gated to SMC for signal-driven new entries.
   KETS/additional strategy signals cannot open a new automatic position.
4. A historical SMC signal older than 180 seconds cannot be used as a new
   entry trigger. It remains visible in history but is not an entry command.
5. Existing cTrader connection, TP/SL, break-even, trailing, opposite-signal
   confirmation, total-profit target, trading hours and other setup are not
   changed.

Important:
- The actual SMC source must explicitly identify its payload as SMC.
- Existing SMC payloads that already contain an SMC marker continue to work.
- This fix does not change the SMC strategy-generation rules.
