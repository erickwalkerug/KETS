KETS AUTO-TRADER — BREAK-EVEN + TRAILING STOP

Server-side cTrader protection has been implemented.
- Break-Even and Trailing Stop controls are persisted on the cTrader account.
- When protection is enabled, the existing signal Stop Loss is used as the initial risk anchor.
- Break-Even triggers automatically at 1 initial risk unit and moves SL to exact entry.
- cTrader native trailing is enabled after BE when both are ON; if trailing alone is ON, native trailing is enabled from the original SL.
- No 1:1 or 1:2 input is exposed.
- Existing KETS/SMC/Strong Reversal entry logic remains unchanged.
- Auto-Trader's opposite-signal exit remains in place.
- When protection is enabled, an initial broker-side SL is attached because BE/trailing require a real SL risk anchor.
- The existing signal TP calculation is not altered.
