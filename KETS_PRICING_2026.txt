KETS PRICING

ONE SUBSCRIPTION — 30 DAYS
Uganda: UGX 500,000
International clients: USD $150

Access expires 30 days after the administrator approves a verified manual payment.
Country/currency is determined from the account's saved country:
- Uganda (UG): UGX, with MTN or Airtel mobile money selection.
- Other countries: USD, international payment through Pesapal.

Manual payment workflow:
- Users submit a bank/mobile-money transaction reference.
- Server-side amount and currency selection.
- Access is granted only after the server verifies the transaction status with Pesapal.
- Pending manual payments do not activate access until admin approval.
- Configure KETS_DEVELOPER_USERNAME and KETS_DEVELOPER_PASSWORD in Render for the admin approval dashboard.
- Verify each bank/mobile-money payment manually before approving it.

All paid subscriptions use the single 30-day duration. Retired plan IDs are not offered for new purchases; any existing completed payment record is evaluated using the 30-day duration.
