KETS PRICING

ONE SUBSCRIPTION — 30 DAYS
Uganda: UGX 500,000
International clients: USD $150

Access expires 30 days after the payment is confirmed by Pesapal.
Country/currency is determined from the account's saved country:
- Uganda (UG): UGX, with MTN or Airtel mobile money selection.
- Other countries: USD, international payment through Pesapal.

Pesapal integration:
- Hosted checkout through Pesapal API 3.0.
- Server-side amount and currency selection.
- Access is granted only after the server verifies the transaction status with Pesapal.
- The callback and IPN endpoints are used to confirm payment; pending/failed payments do not activate access.
- Render must have PESAPAL_CONSUMER_KEY, PESAPAL_CONSUMER_SECRET, PESAPAL_IPN_ID,
  PESAPAL_ENVIRONMENT, and KETS_PUBLIC_URL configured correctly.
- Register https://kets.onrender.com/api/payments/ipn with Pesapal and set the returned
  IPN ID as PESAPAL_IPN_ID. Confirm the public URL matches the deployed service.
- Test a Uganda UGX payment and an international USD payment in Pesapal before launch.

All paid subscriptions use the single 30-day duration. Retired plan IDs are not offered for new purchases; any existing completed payment record is evaluated using the 30-day duration.
