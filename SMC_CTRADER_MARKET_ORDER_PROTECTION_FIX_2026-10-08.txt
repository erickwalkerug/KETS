KETS SMC cTRADER MARKET-ORDER PROTECTION FIX — 2026-10-08

Problem found
- The automatic SMC execution path was putting absolute stopLoss and takeProfit fields directly into the initial cTrader MARKET order.
- cTrader Open API does not support absolute stopLoss/takeProfit fields on MARKET orders.
- This could cause the market entry request itself to be rejected, leaving 0 open trades even though the SMC signal had arrived.

Fix
- The initial cTrader MARKET order now sends only the market entry.
- KETS keeps the exact SMC Stop Loss and Take Profit values from the source signal.
- Immediately after the market position is opened, KETS applies those exact SL/TP values to the position through cTrader's position-protection operation.
- KETS then reads the actual cTrader position and verifies both SL and TP.
- If broker-side protection cannot be confirmed for an automatic trade, KETS attempts to close the automatic position rather than intentionally leaving it unprotected.

Rules preserved
- SMC remains an independent signal source.
- SMC structure is evaluated by the trading bot, not recalculated by KETS.
- No second candle/BOS/order-block execution gate was added.
- SMC source identity remains explicit; ordinary BUY/SELL signals are not treated as SMC.
- Existing Auto-Trader direction/reversal controls remain unchanged.
- cTrader remains the authority for the actual broker-side SL/TP.
