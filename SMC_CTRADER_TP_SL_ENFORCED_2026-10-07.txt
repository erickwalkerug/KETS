KETS SMC cTrader TP/SL ENFORCEMENT — 2026-10-07

CHANGE MADE
- SMC Auto-Trader now requires BOTH the signal Stop Loss and Take Profit.
- Both levels are sent directly in the cTrader MARKET order payload.
- cTrader is the system that actually places the broker-side SL/TP.
- KETS does not treat TP as informational for an automatic SMC trade.
- An automatic SMC trade is refused if either SL or TP is missing.
- The existing SMC independent execution gate remains unchanged.
- Existing opposite-direction confirmation, duplicate-signal handling, profit target,
  break-even, trailing, and other Auto-Trader controls remain unchanged.
- Manual trading behaviour remains unchanged.

IMPORTANT
The broker/cTrader can still reject an order if the supplied SL/TP violates the
broker's minimum distance, price precision, market state, or other trading rules.
In that case KETS records the cTrader rejection instead of opening an unprotected
automatic SMC position.
