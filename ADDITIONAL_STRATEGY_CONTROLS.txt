KETS ADDITIONAL STRATEGY CONTROLS
=================================
The Auto Trader button page now includes ON/OFF controls for:
Trend Following, Breakout, Mean Reversion, Momentum, Price Action,
Support & Resistance, Supply & Demand, VWAP, Moving Average Cross, MACD,
RSI, Bollinger Bands, Fibonacci, Scalping, and News / Volatility.

The selection is stored per connected cTrader account in
strategy_selection_json. When at least one additional strategy is selected,
the Auto Trader uses the independent strategy-candidate feed from the
trading bot. When none are selected, the existing KETS Signal History
execution path remains unchanged.

SMC and Strong Reversal controls remain separate.
