GMMA PAGE + AUTOTRADER REVERSAL EXECUTION — 2026-11-10

- BUY entry: short-group average crosses above long-group average.
- SELL entry: short-group average crosses below long-group average.
- Exit: close KETS automatic positions on a confirmed opposite GMMA crossover; when Auto-Trader is ON, the opposite reversal can then open the new direction.
